Ontario Limitations Act, 2002 - Summary
The basic limitation period is two years. A claim is discovered on the 
earlier of the day the person knew or ought reasonably to have known 
that the injury, loss or damage had occurred, was caused by an act or 
omission, and that a proceeding would be an appropriate remedy.

The ultimate limitation period is 15 years from the date the act or 
omission took place...
